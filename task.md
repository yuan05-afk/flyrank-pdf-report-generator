Goal
Build the workshop's pipeline yourself: query your data, render it into a PDF report, and generate it as a background job — on demand now, on a schedule for stretch.

Purpose
"Generate a report" is the most classic background job in software — every SaaS ships it. It exercises everything from the last four weeks in one feature: SQL aggregation, artifact handling (store and link, don't pass 20 MB around), and your A7 job pattern. It also demos beautifully.
